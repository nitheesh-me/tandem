How to

How to watch a movie together online with friends

You can watch a movie together online with friends in other places, each on their own screen, with Tandem Watch Party. It is free and needs no account.

One of you needs the movie as a video file, a folder, a magnet link or a .torrent file. The others do not need a copy.

Steps

Watch a movie together in five steps

  1. Install Tandem

    Everyone who watches needs Tandem, on Linux, Windows or Android. Public downloads open soon, and the download section lists each one.

  2. Open a room and add the movie

    Press Open room on the home screen, then drop the movie into the window or press Choose files. You can also paste a magnet link.

  3. Send the invite

    Press Copy invite and paste the link into any chat app. Anyone with the link can join, so send it only to the people you are watching with.

  4. Your friends join

    They open the link and press Join room. The app always asks before it joins.

  5. Press play

    When everyone is in, anyone presses play and every screen starts on the same frame. A pause or a skip happens for everyone too.

One copy is enough

If a friend does not have the movie

A friend who joins without the movie sees which file the room needs. They can press Stream from stations to get it from the others as they watch, or I have a copy to use their own, which Tandem checks is the same file.

Just for you

Subtitles and sound for each person

Each person can open Tracks under the picture and choose their own audio language and subtitles, or add a subtitle file. Nobody else sees the change, and the room stays in sync.

Tips

Tips for a good movie night

  • Set a start time. The host can set a Showtime countdown, a few minutes ahead or at a time of day, and everyone starts together.
  • On a slow connection, download first. Download ahead fetches the whole movie before the watch, and a Station on a slow link gets a smaller stream rather than holding everyone up.
  • Nobody gets left behind. If someone's picture stalls, the room waits up to 20 seconds for them, or less if you press Continue without them.
  • Make it a regular thing. Keep the people you watch with as a friend group, with a schedule and a reminder before each movie night.
  • Watch what you may. Please share and download only media you have the right to.
