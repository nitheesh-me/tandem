Tandem, the art piece (docs/site/art)

Code: everything in art/js, art.css and build.py is written for this site. It uses WebGL2 and WebAudio from the
browser and no libraries: nothing from npm or a CDN is shipped, and nothing is loaded from anywhere but this site.

  - js/wall.js      the wall of screens, built from the Wall mark's geometry (img/icon.svg) and cut the same way,
                    with the order in which the invite and the film reach each screen
  - js/shaders.js   the film (a flowing contour map with the playhead), the screens, hollow frames, the film's
                    fragments, the links, the pointer's field of lateness, bloom and the final picture
  - js/main.js      the nine acts, the camera's path, input, the names, the room clock, the closing words
  - js/audio.js     the live mix of the score: phase offsets per part, the lock, the fermata, the tape stop

The score (art/score/techno, the default, and art/score/cine, at ?score=cine)
  Composed for this page and rendered offline by target/team/roster/site-art/sound/render.py. Every sound in it
  is synthesised there (oscillators, noise, additive plucks, FM bells, a state-variable filter); no samples or
  recordings by anyone else are used. The files are Ogg Opus (and AAC in MP4 for browsers without Opus),
  encoded with ffmpeg. The tools used to make them, none of which is shipped with the site:
    - NumPy 2.3.5 (BSD-3-Clause) and SciPy 1.16.3 (BSD-3-Clause): synthesis and filters
    - pedalboard 0.9.17 by Spotify (GPL-3.0): reverb, delay, chorus, compression and filters, used offline only;
      only the rendered audio is in the site, not the library
    - FFmpeg with libopus (BSD-3-Clause) and its AAC encoder: encoding

Fonts, shared with the rest of the site (../fonts, see ../fonts/LICENSES.txt), all under the SIL Open Font
License 1.1: JetBrains Mono (as site-mono-*), Iosevka (as site-iosevka-900) and Inter (as site-inter-400). The
place names in Arabic, Japanese, Korean and Devanagari use the visitor's own system fonts.

The people are made-up handles. No film footage is used; the film every screen plays is drawn by a shader.
still.webp, shown when WebGL is missing, is a frame of the piece itself.
