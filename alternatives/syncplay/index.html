Syncplay alternative

A Syncplay alternative that needs no server

Syncplay keeps video players such as mpv, VLC and MPC-HC in sync through a Syncplay server, a public one or your own [12] [13], and everyone needs their own copy of the film [13].

Tandem Watch Party is a free app that needs no server to pick or run. The devices in a room connect to each other directly, with public relays only when a direct connection fails, and only one person needs the file.

No server, one copy

What changes with Tandem

  • One copy is enough. Friends without the film stream it from the room, and every piece is checked as it arrives.
  • Copies are compared. When people bring their own, Tandem marks each one Same file, Probably same, Same length or Different file before anyone presses play.
  • The screens stay closer. Syncplay's defaults slow a player at 1.5 seconds behind and rewind it at 4 seconds ahead [14]. Tandem nudges each player a touch faster or slower, and in tests on one computer the screens stayed about 10 ms apart.
  • Phones, tablets and TVs join too. Tandem runs on Android and Android TV as well as Linux and Windows.
  • Your own subtitles and audio stay yours, as in Syncplay, and the room stays in sync while you change them.

Fair to Syncplay

What Syncplay does better

  • It works inside the player you already like, such as mpv, VLC, MPC-HC or IINA [12] [13]. Tandem has its own player.
  • It runs on macOS and BSD as well as Windows and Linux [12]. Tandem has no Mac release yet.
  • It has been around since 2012 [13], and it is light: it never converts a film, while Tandem's conversion of a format a screen cannot play is heavy on a computer without a working graphics chip.

Try it on your next film night

One of you opens a room in Tandem and adds the film. Press Copy invite, send the link in any chat app, and when your friends have joined, anyone presses play.
