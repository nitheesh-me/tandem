Teleparty alternative

A free Teleparty alternative for your own video files

Teleparty is a browser extension and app that keeps Netflix, Disney+, Hulu, Prime Video, YouTube and HBO Max in sync for a group [1]. Tandem Watch Party is a free alternative for a different case: the video is a file you already have, and you want to watch it in sync with friends somewhere else.

Tandem runs on Linux, Windows, Android and Android TV, and a friend on a computer can join from the invite in a browser.

Your own files

When Tandem is the better fit

  • The film is a file, a folder, a magnet link or a .torrent file, not something on a streaming service.
  • Only one of you has it. The others stream it from the room, so nobody needs a subscription or a copy of their own.
  • Nobody wants to sign up. Tandem has no account at all. Teleparty needs its extension or app, and a Google sign-in for Premium [1].
  • Each person wants their own subtitles and audio. In Tandem everyone picks their own, and the room stays in sync.
  • Someone watches on a TV. Tandem runs on Android TV, where the remote's play key plays for the whole room.
  • It costs nothing. Tandem is free. Teleparty is free too, with a Premium plan for $6.59 a month that adds more services, voice and video [1].

Chat and reactions while the film plays

Fair to Teleparty

When Teleparty is the better fit

  • You want to watch a streaming service together. Tandem cannot play Netflix, Disney+ or any other streaming service.
  • You want to talk. Teleparty has voice and video with Premium [1]. Tandem has text chat and reactions only.
  • The room is big. Teleparty says it takes rooms of "up to 1000+" [1]. Tandem suits a group of friends as full members, and a bigger audience can only watch along.
  • Someone is on an iPhone or a Mac. Teleparty has an iOS app [1]. Tandem has no iPhone or Mac release yet.

Start a watch party with Tandem

One of you opens a room in Tandem and adds the film, presses Copy invite and sends the link in any chat app. The others open it and press Join room, and when anyone presses play, every screen starts on the same frame.
